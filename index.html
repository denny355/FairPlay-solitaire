Tap a face-up card or stack to auto-play, or drag it to a destination. Foundations accept any Ace in any empty slot. When all remaining cards are face up in the seven columns and the draw/waste piles are empty, Auto Finish moves them to the foundations for you. The timer stops when you complete the game or choose Give Up. Completing within 5 minutes earns a time bonus of 10 points per second remaining.
