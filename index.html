♠ FairPlay Solitaire

Tap a card, then tap where you want to move it.

Tap the stock to draw. Tap a face-up card (or a stack) and then its destination. Build tableau piles in alternating colors, descending from King to Ace. Build foundations by suit from Ace to King. Tap the empty stock to recycle the waste. Double-tap a top card to send it to a foundation when possible.
